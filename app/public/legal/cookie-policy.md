# Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

## What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your browser when you visit a website.

## Cookies We Use

Code Siren uses only **essential cookies** necessary for the Service to function:

| Cookie Name | Purpose | Type | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| `code_siren_token` | Authentication — stores your JWT session token | Essential (httpOnly, Secure, SameSite=Strict) | 7 days (session duration) |

## What We Don't Use

- ❌ No tracking cookies
- ❌ No advertising cookies
- ❌ No third-party analytics cookies
- ❌ No social media cookies

## Managing Cookies

- **Essential cookies** cannot be disabled — the Service requires them to authenticate you
- Your JWT token is stored as an httpOnly cookie, meaning JavaScript cannot access it (protection against XSS)
- You can clear the cookie by logging out, which deletes your session

## Browser Settings

You can control cookies through your browser settings:
- Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies
- Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies
- Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Cookies

## Changes

If we add new cookies in the future, we will update this policy and notify you.
