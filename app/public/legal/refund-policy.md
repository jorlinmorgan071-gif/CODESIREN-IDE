# Refund Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

## Free Tier

Code Siren offers a free tier with no payment required. No refunds are applicable for free usage.

## Paid Plans

If we introduce paid plans in the future:

1. **Monthly subscriptions**: Refundable within 7 days of the most recent charge, provided you have not exceeded 100 API calls in that billing period.

2. **Annual subscriptions**: Refundable within 30 days of purchase, pro-rated based on months used.

3. **One-time purchases**: Refundable within 14 days if the feature has not been used more than 10 times.

## How to Request a Refund

1. Delete your account via `DELETE /api/auth/account` (if you want to close the account)
2. Contact us through the GitHub repository with your account email and transaction ID
3. Refunds are processed within 5-10 business days to the original payment method

## Non-Refundable Items

- API usage fees for calls already made
- Setup or configuration fees
- Custom development work

## Chargebacks

Please contact us before initiating a chargeback — we can usually resolve issues faster through direct communication.
